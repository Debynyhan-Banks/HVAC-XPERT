# License and Rights Review

## Status

Open. No manufacturer source is currently approved for automated collection or public redistribution.

The read-only review completed on 2026-08-02 found that the posted North American terms prohibit automated systems from monitoring or copying covered site content. Written permission or a separate agreement is required before crawler use.

## Required determinations per source

- Who owns the source and its contents?
- Is access public, authenticated, licensed, or partner-only?
- Do the terms permit automated discovery, downloading, storage, and factual extraction?
- What does the robots policy allow?
- May source binaries be retained, and for how long?
- May complete documents, excerpts, tables, images, or diagrams be redistributed?
- May derived factual data and original redraws be published?
- Is attribution required?
- Is a removal, expiration, or data-refresh process required?
- Do regional or contractual restrictions apply?

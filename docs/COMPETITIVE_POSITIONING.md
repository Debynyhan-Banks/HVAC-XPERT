# Competitive Positioning

## Status

Historical market analysis. It does not define the active build sequence after ADR-020. Current priorities are the single-owner local-first threads in `PERSONAL_USE_PIVOT.md` and `../ROADMAP.md`.

## Purpose

This document converts a dated review of adjacent HVAC products into product-priority decisions for HVAC XPERT. Competitor descriptions are market references, not technical sources for manufacturer facts or simulator behavior.

## Positioning statement

HVAC XPERT is a model- and revision-specific HVAC/R diagnostic copilot and training simulator that guides a technician to the next safe, discriminating test and shows the reviewed manufacturer evidence behind every technical recommendation.

## Historical initial market wedge

The pilot does not attempt to win through the largest manual library, the broadest hardware ecosystem, or the most immersive 3D course catalog. It proves depth on one technically complex inverter equipment family by completing one evidence-backed diagnostic case from complaint through traceable summary.

The field diagnostic workflow is the primary pilot experience. Training remains a required product mode and reuses the same approved equipment records, procedures, deterministic rules, and diagnostic path.

## Intended differentiation

One reviewed equipment knowledge package powers:

`technical reference -> interactive schematic -> field diagnosis -> deterministic training simulation`

The product combines:

- exact model and revision applicability;
- claim-level source and review traceability;
- explicit unknown and conflict handling;
- deterministic comparison and simulation rules;
- clear separation of actual, reference, simulated, and AI-generated information;
- one-next-test-at-a-time field guidance; and
- a traceable case record that supports technician escalation and training remediation.

AI organizes and explains approved evidence. It does not replace the knowledge package, deterministic rules, technician observations, or HVAC technical approval.

## Pilot product priorities

### Build now

1. Complete one pilot diagnostic case from equipment confirmation through closure or escalation.
2. Present one recommended test at a time with rationale, safety, points, meter mode, expected result, and result entry in one view.
3. Keep detailed provenance and validation information available on demand without making the primary field workflow difficult to scan.
4. Evaluate technician-entered results deterministically and show supporting, contradicting, and missing evidence.
5. Produce a concise, traceable service or escalation summary.
6. Reuse the same case path as the first reviewed training scenario.

### Prepare for later

- Nameplate model and serial OCR with mandatory technician confirmation
- Offline-capable field access with safe knowledge-version synchronization
- User-authorized read-only import from compatible meters and probes
- Remote lead-technician case review and escalation
- Historical case and known-good comparison with exact equipment applicability
- Approved service-bulletin and parts-applicability alerts
- Voice-assisted intake with visible confirmation

Imported instrument data remains a technician observation with device, time, unit, and operating-condition metadata. Read-only import does not authorize equipment control, unattended monitoring, or inferred measurements.

### Do not build for the pilot

- General photorealistic 3D equipment models
- A full thermodynamic physics engine
- Broad manufacturer coverage
- Foundation-model training on manufacturer PDFs
- Live equipment control or unattended data collection
- AR equipment recognition
- Dispatch, invoicing, payment, inventory, or parts-purchasing systems
- An unrestricted crawler
- A large general HVAC course catalog

## Field experience principles

- Start with equipment identity, complaint, and immediate safety state.
- Show one primary next action rather than an unranked list of procedures.
- Explain why the test matters before asking for a result.
- Keep the actual reading, expected reference, and evaluation visually distinct.
- Put advanced provenance, raw identifiers, and reviewer metadata one action away.
- Never hide unknown applicability, missing evidence, conflict, or a required stop.
- Let a technician escalate without completing unsupported steps.
- Optimize for gloved, mobile, low-attention field use before adding visual spectacle.

## Competitive proof measures

The pilot should establish evidence for the product position through:

- time from case creation to the next approved discriminating test;
- number of completed versus skipped required safety steps;
- agreement between the application path and HVAC reviewer decisions;
- number of unsupported or misapplied recommendations, with a target of zero;
- number of unnecessary or repeated tests in a reviewed case;
- completeness of the service or escalation summary;
- technician ability to locate supporting evidence; and
- learner improvement when the same case is replayed in training mode.

The product should not claim reduced callbacks, faster diagnosis, or improved first-time fix rate until a controlled field evaluation supports those outcomes.

Hello,

I am developing HVAC XPERT, an HVAC/R technician-training and diagnostic platform. The initial pilot is limited to the Amana/Goodman S-Series inverter family, beginning with model `ASXS6S4810AA`.

I am requesting written guidance and, if available, permission for the following controlled uses of publicly available manufacturer technical documents:

1. Automated discovery of public document links on specifically approved Daikin Comfort Technologies, Goodman, and Amana pages.

3. Private storage of source documents for internal extraction, validation, and audit purposes.
4. Extraction of factual equipment data such as model applicability, components, connector identifiers, service measurements, fault codes, and operating sequences.
5. Creation of original interactive SVG schematic redraws and original training explanations derived from validated facts.
6. Publication of approved factual records with source attribution and validation status.

HVAC XPERT would not publicly redistribute complete manufacturer manuals, copied diagrams, credential-controlled materials, confidential materials, trademarks as branding, or substantial source excerpts without separate express permission.

The system would respect approved domains, robots policies, crawl delays, rate limits, authentication boundaries, removal requests, and any retention or attribution requirements you specify. AI extraction would not be treated as manufacturer-verified, and human technical review would be required before publication.
